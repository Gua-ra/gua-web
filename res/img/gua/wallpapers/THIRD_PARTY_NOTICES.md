# Third-party notices

## Photo wallpapers

The photo wallpapers, `photo-*.webp` in this folder and their gallery thumbnails in `thumbnails/`, are not under the AGPL or the GPL. Each keeps its own licence, listed below. The legal code is in `licenses/CC0-1.0.txt` and `licenses/CC-BY-4.0.txt`.

Every photo was cropped to the 1290:2796 phone shape, resized to 1290x2796 where needed and re-encoded as WebP. Its colours were converted to sRGB and its metadata removed, and an XMP packet names the author, licence and source. `tools/wallpapers/bake.mjs` in gua-android bakes them from its `photos.json`, which records each original's URL, sha256 and crop. `scripts/gua/sync-wallpapers.mjs` copies them here unchanged and resizes each to 168x364 for its thumbnail.
